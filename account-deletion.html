Account & Data Deletion

App: Lihenium: Scan PDF & Vault
Last Updated: March 4, 2026

This page explains how you can delete your data and revoke access associated with Lihenium. We aim to handle deletion requests in accordance with applicable privacy laws.

1. How Lihenium Handles Your Data

Lihenium does not run a custom backend that stores your scanned documents or vault files on our own servers. Your documents are stored on your device, and any cloud backup (if you enable it) is stored in your own Google Drive.

Sign-in (if you choose to use it) uses Google Sign-In (OAuth). This is not a separate “Lihenium account” created on our servers.

2. Self-Service Deletion (Instant)

You can remove your data immediately using these steps:

  • Delete local app data: Uninstall the app, or go to Settings → Apps → Lihenium → Storage → Clear data. (This removes locally stored scans and settings on that device.)
  • Delete Google Drive backups (if enabled): Open Google Drive and delete the Lihenium backup folder/files you created.
  • Revoke Google account access: Visit Google Account permissions , find Lihenium, and remove access.

3. Contact Us for Help (Optional)

If you need help finding backups, revoking access, or confirming what you removed locally, contact us:

Email: lihenium.app@gmail.com
Subject: Account/Data Deletion Help
Include: the Google email you used for sign-in (if any) and a short description of what you want deleted.

Because we do not host your documents on our own servers, most deletion is completed by the self-service steps above.

4. What Data May Still Exist (Third-Party Services)

Some data related to app quality and billing may be processed by Google services:

  • Google Play billing records: Purchases and subscription records are processed by Google Play and may be retained by Google for tax, accounting, and fraud prevention.
  • Crash reports (if enabled): Crash reports may be processed by Google/Firebase services to help improve app stability. We do not store your documents in crash reporting.
  • Ads (if enabled): Ads are delivered by Google AdMob. Ad-related identifiers are handled by Google’s ad services on the device.

For ad identifiers specifically, users can reset or limit them in Android settings (the exact path varies by device).

5. Processing Time

If you contact us for assistance, we typically respond within 7–14 days. Some items (like billing records) are controlled by Google Play and cannot be deleted by us.

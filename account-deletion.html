Account & Data Deletion Policy

App: Lihenium: Scan PDF & Vault
Last Updated: March 4, 2026

At Lihenium, we respect your privacy and your right to control your personal data, in compliance with global privacy regulations including the GDPR (EU/UK) and CCPA/CPRA (California).

1. How Lihenium Handles Your Data

Lihenium does not maintain a centralized server or database for user accounts or scanned documents. Authentication is handled securely on your device via Google Sign-In (OAuth), and backups are stored in your own personal Google Drive.

2. Self-Service Data Deletion (Instant)

Because we do not store your data on external servers, you have complete power to instantly delete your data and revoke our access without waiting for our team:

  • Delete Local Data: Clear the app data in your device settings or uninstall the Lihenium app. This removes all locally stored scans and preferences.
  • Delete Cloud Backups: Open your Google Drive, search for the Lihenium backup folder, and delete it to remove your scanned documents from the cloud.
  • Revoke Account Access: Go to your Google Account Security Settings, find "Connections to third-party apps and services," select Lihenium, and click "Delete all connections."

3. Submit a Manual Deletion Request

If you prefer that we manually purge any associated analytics, ad identifiers, or subscription-related connection data we may hold, you can submit a formal Right to Erasure request.

  1. Send an email from the exact Google account you used to sign in to the app.
  2. Use the subject line: Account/Data Deletion Request
  3. Include the following information in your email:
    • Your Google email address used in the app
    • App name: Lihenium: Scan PDF & Vault
    • Request statement: "I request the deletion of all my user data and account connections."

Submit requests to: lihenium.app@gmail.com

4. What Data is Retained?

While we will delete all applicable identifiers upon request, certain data must be retained for legal and security purposes:

  • Financial Records: Payment and subscription transaction records are processed and managed entirely by Google Play. Google retains these records for legal, tax, and auditing compliance.
  • Aggregated Crash Data: Anonymous, non-identifiable crash reports (via Firebase Crashlytics) may remain in aggregated form to ensure app stability.

5. Processing Time

We process all manual data deletion requests within 7 to 14 days of receipt, well within the 30-day requirement mandated by international privacy laws.

5. Local-First Processing and Storage

+

+ Lihenium is built as a local-first document utility. In normal usage, your scans, PDFs, edits, + previews, OCR output, and vault-protected files remain on your device or in storage locations that + you choose. +

+
    +
  • Document scanning, image processing, PDF review, and most file handling occur on-device.
    • +
  • ML Kit-based OCR, QR scanning, and subject segmentation are designed to run on-device.
    • +
  • Optional translation features use on-device ML Kit translation models, although the App may download language models when needed.
    • +
  • Vault content is intended to remain protected on your device using locally controlled credentials and security features.
    • +
+
+ If you forget your vault passphrase, PIN, or recovery details, private vault content may become + inaccessible. Please store your credentials carefully. +

8. Google Sign-In, Drive Backup, and ML Features

+ +

8.1 Google Sign-In

+

+ Google Sign-In is optional. If you sign in, the App may locally keep basic account information such as + your email, display name, user ID, and profile picture URI in order to restore your session and enable + backup-related features. +

+ +

8.2 Google Drive backup

+

+ If you enable backup or restore, the App uses Google Drive's appDataFolder area. This + is a hidden, app-specific storage space in your Google account that is intended for application data and + is not part of your regular visible Drive files area. +

+
    +
  • Backups are created only when you trigger backup or restore actions.
    • +
  • We do not receive your Google password.
    • +
  • Your backup files are stored in Google infrastructure under Google's own policies and security controls.
    • +
+ +

8.3 ML Kit and translation model downloads

+

+ The App may use Google ML Kit for OCR, language identification, translation, QR scanning, and related + on-device features. According to Google's ML Kit documentation, input data is processed on-device, + but ML Kit APIs may occasionally contact Google servers for model downloads, updates, compatibility + information, and performance or utilization metrics. +

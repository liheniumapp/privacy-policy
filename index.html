2. Information We Handle

2.1 Content you create or import

  • Scanned pages, imported images, PDFs, OCR text, edited files, exports, and file metadata.
  • Vault content and vault-related local settings used to protect your private files.
  • Files you intentionally open through the camera, Android file picker, share sheet, or supported import flows.

2.2 Optional account information

If you choose to sign in with Google, the App may access and locally store basic account details such as your email address, display name, profile picture, and Google account identifier so that optional account-based features can work properly.

2.3 App settings and technical state

  • Theme, language, file preferences, app lock settings, and related local configuration.
  • Backup state, restore state, processing status, and other technical data needed to run features you request.

2.4 Diagnostics, purchases, and advertising data

If these services are enabled in your version of the App, we may receive limited technical or status data from third-party providers such as Google Play Billing, Google Mobile Ads, and Firebase Crashlytics.

3. How We Use Information

  • To provide scanning, OCR, PDF tools, document organization, secure vault, export, and related file features.
  • To save, restore, back up, and manage your files when you choose those actions.
  • To support optional sign-in and Google Drive backup features.
  • To verify premium purchases or subscription entitlements handled through Google Play.
  • To improve reliability, investigate crashes, and respond to support requests.
  • To comply with legal obligations and protect users, the App, and third-party services from misuse.
We do not operate our own document-processing server for the App’s core scanning and editing functions. Most core processing is intended to happen locally on your device.

4. Local Processing and Storage

Lihenium is designed primarily as a local-first app. In normal use, your scans, PDFs, OCR output, vault files, previews, and edits remain on your device or in storage locations that you choose.

  • Most document processing and file handling occur on-device.
  • Backups are only created when you actively choose to use backup or restore features.
  • Files may leave your device only when you explicitly export, share, print, email, or back them up.
Please keep your device, vault passcode, PIN, and recovery details secure. If you lose access credentials, some protected content may become inaccessible.

The permissions requested may vary depending on Android version and the features you choose to use.

FOREGROUND_SERVICE To keep user-requested long-running tasks active. Backup, restore, or extended processing.

6. Google Services and Third Parties

Depending on the features enabled in your version of the App, some third-party services may be used.

6.1 Google Sign-In

Google Sign-In is optional. If you sign in, your basic Google account information may be used so the App can maintain your session and support account-based features.

6.2 Google Drive backup

If you use backup or restore, the App may store backup data in your Google Drive appDataFolder, which is intended to be an app-specific storage area within your Google account.

6.3 ML Kit features

The App may use Google ML Kit for features such as OCR, QR scanning, language identification, translation, or document-related processing. These features are generally designed to run on-device, although some model downloads or service interactions may occur depending on the feature.

6.4 Ads, billing, and diagnostics

If enabled, the free version of the App may display ads through Google Mobile Ads. Premium purchases may be handled through Google Play Billing. Public releases may also use Firebase Crashlytics to help identify app crashes, ANRs, and major stability issues.

7. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be shared only in limited situations such as:

  • With service providers or platforms you choose to use, such as Google Sign-In, Google Drive backup, Google Play Billing, Google Mobile Ads, ML Kit, or Firebase Crashlytics.
  • With other apps or services when you intentionally export, share, print, email, or open a file outside the App.
  • When required by law, legal process, or legitimate safety and security needs.
  • As part of a business transfer, merger, acquisition, or asset sale, if ever applicable.

8. Retention and Deletion

  • Local files: remain on your device until you delete them, clear app data, or uninstall the App.
  • Local settings and session data: remain until you sign out, clear app data, or uninstall the App.
  • Google Drive backups: remain until you remove or replace them in your Google account environment.
  • Third-party diagnostics, ad, or billing data: may be retained by the relevant provider according to that provider’s own policies.

9. Your Choices

  • Use most core document features without signing in.
  • Grant, deny, or revoke permissions in Android system settings.
  • Sign out of your Google account inside the App if you no longer want account-based features.
  • Delete local files, exports, backups, and vault items when you choose.
  • Manage connected Google account permissions and privacy controls through your Google account settings.
  • Uninstall the App or clear its local storage to remove on-device app data.

10. Security

We use reasonable measures designed to help protect information handled by the App, including local storage protections, vault-related controls, and platform security features made available by Android and supporting services.

  • Vault and app-lock features are intended to add local protection for sensitive files.
  • Biometric features depend on your device and your settings.
  • No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

11. Children’s Privacy

The App is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in a way intended to profile or target them.

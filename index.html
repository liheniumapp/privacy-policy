Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Lihenium Studio ("we", "us", "our") handles information when you use Lihenium: Scan PDF & Vault (the "App").

Quick Summary

  • We do not run our own server to store your documents by default.
  • Your PDFs/images/scans/vault files stay on your device unless you choose backup/export/share actions.
  • Google Sign-In and Google Drive backup are optional.
  • Ads and in-app purchases are provided through Google services.

2) Data We Process

A) Data you provide or control

  • Documents, images, and scans you import/open/edit in the App.
  • Vault content and metadata stored locally on your device.
  • Google account details (such as email/account identifier) only if you sign in.
  • Support emails you send to us.

B) Data collected automatically (via device/SDKs)

  • Crash diagnostics and technical logs to improve stability.
  • Ad-related identifiers and interaction signals (for ad serving/measurement), depending on your device settings and consent controls.
  • Purchase status from Google Play Billing (for premium entitlement checks).

3) Permissions We Request and Why

Permission Why it is needed
CAMERA Capture document and image scans.
FLASHLIGHT Torch control while scanning.
READ_MEDIA_IMAGES, READ_MEDIA_VIDEO, READ_MEDIA_AUDIO, READ_MEDIA_VISUAL_USER_SELECTED Open/import media/files selected by you (Android 13+ behavior varies by picker flow and OS).

FOREGROUND_SERVICE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_DATA_SYNC Long-running user-initiated tasks (for example, batch processing/backup flows).
VIBRATE Haptic feedback in supported flows.

4) How We Use Data

  • Provide scanning, OCR, PDF tools, image tools, vault, and document management features.
  • Enable optional Google Sign-In and optional Google Drive backup/restore.
  • Deliver ads in the free experience and process subscriptions/purchases.
  • Monitor reliability, fix bugs, and prevent abuse.

5) Cloud Backup and Account Services

If you enable backup, the App stores backup content in your Google account storage scope (Google Drive app data area).

  • You can sign out/disable backup at any time in the App.
  • We do not receive your Google password.
  • Google handles storage infrastructure under Google's own policies.

6) Ads and Purchases

  • Ads: The App may show banner/native/rewarded ads via Google AdMob (or Google ad services).
  • Purchases: Subscription and payment processing is handled by Google Play Billing. We do not store your card details.

7) Secure Vault and Encryption

  • Vault files are intended to stay protected on your device.
  • Vault password/PIN is controlled by you. We cannot recover private vault content if credentials are lost.
  • Optional backups/export actions are initiated by you.

8) Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal data. We share limited data only when necessary:

  • With service providers used by the App (for example Google Play services, Google Sign-In, Google Drive APIs, crash diagnostics, ads, billing).
  • When required by law, regulation, legal process, or to protect rights/safety.
  • During a merger/acquisition/business transfer (with appropriate policy updates).

9) Data Retention

  • Local files remain until you delete them.
  • Cloud backups remain until deleted by you or overwritten by backup operations.
  • Crash/ads/purchase telemetry retention is managed by the respective third-party service providers.

10) Your Choices

  • Grant/deny runtime permissions from Android settings.
  • Sign in/out of Google account in the App.
  • Enable/disable backup features.
  • Control ad personalization through Google/Android settings where available.
  • Delete local files/vault content from the App.

11) Children's Privacy

The App is for a general audience and not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe such information was provided, contact us.

12) Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards. No method of storage/transmission is 100% secure. Please also secure your device and credentials.

13) International Processing

Some third-party providers may process data in multiple countries according to their legal terms and safeguards.

14) Third-Party Services

The App may rely on services such as Google Play Services, Google Sign-In, Google Drive API, Google Play Billing, Google AdMob, and Firebase components. Their handling of data is governed by their own policies.

15) Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will publish the updated version with a revised date.

Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how Lihenium Studio ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use our mobile application Lihenium: PDF Scan, Edit & Vault (the "App").

Summary

  • We do not run our own servers to store your personal files.
  • Your documents (PDFs/images/scans/vault files) stay on your device unless you choose cloud backup.
  • The App uses Google Sign-In and Google Drive only if you choose to sign in and use backup/restore.
  • The App shows ads and may offer subscriptions. These services are handled by third-party providers.

1) Who we are

App name: Lihenium: PDF Scan, Edit & Vault
Developer: Lihenium Studio
Contact email: Lihenium.app@gmail.com
Jurisdiction: India

2) Information we collect

A) Information you provide

  • Google Account (optional): If you choose Google Sign-In, we receive basic account information provided by Google (such as your Google account identifier and email address, depending on Google settings and consent).
  • Files you import: PDFs, images, scans, and other documents you choose to import into the App are processed on your device. We do not automatically upload them to our servers.

B) Automatically collected information

  • App diagnostics: We and/or our service providers may collect crash logs, basic device/app information to improve stability and fix bugs.
  • Advertising data: Our ad partners may collect device identifiers and advertising data as permitted by your device settings and applicable law.

3) How we use information

  • To provide core features: scanning, PDF tools, image tools, and secure vault functionality.
  • To enable optional sign-in and cloud backup/restore via Google services.
  • To show ads and measure ad performance.
  • To process subscriptions/purchases and verify entitlement (via Google Play Billing).
  • To improve app quality, debug issues, and enhance user experience.

4) Cloud backup (Google Drive)

The App supports optional backup/restore using Google Drive. If you enable backup, your data is stored in your Google Drive under your Google account.

  • We do not have access to your Google password.
  • We do not operate or control Google Drive’s infrastructure.
  • You can stop using backup at any time by signing out or disabling backup in the App.

Note: In the future, we may add additional cloud providers. If so, this Privacy Policy will be updated.

5) Secure Vault & encryption

  • The Secure Vault is designed to protect your private files. Vault data is stored locally on your device and can be backed up to your Google Drive only if you enable it.
  • The App may allow exporting vault backups as encrypted files. Keep your vault password/PIN safe. If you forget it, recovery may not be possible.
  • We do not receive your vault password/PIN and cannot access your vault contents.

6) Ads

The App displays advertisements provided by third-party ad networks. These providers may collect and use information (such as device identifiers, IP address, and ad interaction data) to show and measure ads, subject to their own privacy policies and your device settings.

If personalized ads are shown, it is controlled by your device and Google settings where applicable.

7) Purchases & subscriptions

If you purchase a subscription or premium features, payments are processed by Google Play. We do not collect or store your payment card details.

Google Play may provide us purchase status (e.g., active/expired) to unlock features.

8) Sharing of information

We may share limited information only in these cases:

  • Service providers (e.g., Google Sign-In, Google Drive, Google Play Billing, ad networks, crash reporting) to operate the App.
  • Legal reasons if required to comply with law, regulation, or valid legal process.
  • Business changes if we are involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets (we will update this policy if needed).

9) Data retention

  • Files you manage in the App remain on your device unless you delete them.
  • Cloud backups remain in your Google Drive until you remove them or disable backup (depending on how the feature is implemented).
  • Diagnostic and advertising data is retained by third-party providers according to their policies.

10) Your choices & controls

  • You can sign out of Google Sign-In from within the App (if available) or via your Google account settings.
  • You can disable backup/restore features at any time.
  • You can limit ad personalization through your device/Google ad settings.
  • You can delete your files and vault data inside the App.

11) Children’s privacy

The App is intended for a general audience and is not designed specifically for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child provided personal information, contact us and we will take reasonable steps to delete it.

12) Security

We use reasonable security measures to protect your information. However, no method of storage or transmission is 100% secure. Please keep your device and vault password/PIN secure.

13) Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version on this page with a new “Last updated” date.

Contact

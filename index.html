2. Information We Handle

2.1 Content you create or import

  • Scanned pages, imported images, PDFs, OCR text, edited files, exports, and file metadata.
  • Vault content and vault-related local settings used to protect your private files.
  • Files you intentionally open through the camera, Android file picker, share sheet, or supported import flows.

2.2 Optional account information

If you choose to sign in with Google, the App may access and locally store basic account details such as your email address, display name, profile picture, and Google account identifier so that optional account-based features can work properly.

2.3 App settings and technical state

  • Theme, language, file preferences, app lock settings, and related local configuration.
  • Backup state, restore state, processing status, and other technical data needed to run features you request.

2.4 Diagnostics, purchases, and advertising data

If these services are enabled in your version of the App, we may receive limited technical or status data from third-party providers such as Google Play Billing, Google Mobile Ads, and Firebase Crashlytics.

3. How We Use Information

  • To provide scanning, OCR, PDF tools, document organization, secure vault, export, and related file features.
  • To save, restore, back up, and manage your files when you choose those actions.
  • To support optional sign-in and Google Drive backup features.
  • To verify premium purchases or subscription entitlements handled through Google Play.
  • To improve reliability, investigate crashes, and respond to support requests.
  • To comply with legal obligations and protect users, the App, and third-party services from misuse.
We do not operate our own document-processing server for the App’s core scanning and editing functions. Most core processing is intended to happen locally on your device.

4. Local Processing and Storage

Lihenium is designed primarily as a local-first app. In normal use, your scans, PDFs, OCR output, vault files, previews, and edits remain on your device or in storage locations that you choose.

  • Most document processing and file handling occur on-device.
  • Backups are only created when you actively choose to use backup or restore features.
  • Files may leave your device only when you explicitly export, share, print, email, or back them up.
Please keep your device, vault passcode, PIN, and recovery details secure. If you lose access credentials, some protected content may become inaccessible.

5. Permissions

The permissions requested may vary depending on Android version and the features you choose to use.

CAMERA

Document capture

Used to scan papers, receipts, notes, and other documents using the camera.

FLASHLIGHT

Low-light assistance

Used to turn on the torch while scanning in dark environments.

READ_MEDIA_IMAGES

Import selected images

Used when you choose photos or images from your device for scanning or PDF creation.

READ_MEDIA_VIDEO / AUDIO

Access selected media

Used only when supported media features require files that you intentionally select.

LEGACY STORAGE

Older Android compatibility

Older Android versions may require storage access for import and export actions.

INTERNET / NETWORK

Online features

Used for sign-in, backup, billing, ads, diagnostics, language model downloads, and connectivity checks.

POST_NOTIFICATIONS

Progress updates

Used to show backup, restore, export, and other long-running task notifications.

USE_BIOMETRIC

Biometric unlock

Used only if you enable biometric protection for the vault or app lock.

FOREGROUND SERVICE

Reliable task execution

Helps Android keep long-running user-requested jobs active and visible while they finish.

VIBRATE

Haptic feedback

Used for supported interaction feedback during scanning or other app actions.

6. Google Services and Third Parties

6.1 Google Sign-In

Google Sign-In is optional. If you choose to use it, the App may locally keep basic account information so it can maintain your sign-in state and enable account-based features.

6.2 Google Drive backup

If you enable backup or restore, the App may use Google Drive’s hidden appDataFolder area for app-specific backup storage. Backups are created only when you choose to use those features.

6.3 Google ML features

The App may use ML-based features for OCR, translation, scanning assistance, language identification, or QR-related functionality. These features are generally intended to work on-device, although some models or supporting components may need to be downloaded.

6.4 Ads, billing, and diagnostics

Depending on what is enabled in your app build, Google Mobile Ads, Google Play Billing, or Firebase Crashlytics may process limited technical data, billing status, crash reports, or advertising-related information under their own policies.

7. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal data. Information may be shared only in limited situations such as:

  • With Google services or other providers that you choose to use inside the App.
  • When you export, print, email, share, or open a file with another app by your own action.
  • When crash reporting, billing, or ad services are enabled and need limited technical data to function.
  • When disclosure is required by law or necessary to protect rights, security, or platform integrity.

8. Retention and Deletion

  • Local files and vault content: remain on your device until you delete them, clear app data, or uninstall the App.
  • Local sign-in state: remains until you sign out, clear app data, or remove the App.
  • Cloud backups: remain until you delete or replace them, or revoke the App’s access where applicable.
  • Third-party service data: may be retained by Google, Firebase, or other providers under their own retention rules.

9. Your Choices

  • Use most core document features without signing in.
  • Grant, deny, or revoke permissions through Android settings.
  • Sign out if you no longer want to use account-based features.
  • Delete local files, vault items, exports, or backups whenever you choose.
  • Manage Google account access, advertising settings, or connected services through your Google settings where relevant.
  • Clear app data or uninstall the App to remove locally stored information.
There is no standalone Lihenium cloud account operated by us. If you use Google features, some privacy controls may need to be managed through your Google account as well as inside the App.

10. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to help protect information handled by the App. This includes local storage protections, Android security features, optional vault protections, and the security controls offered by platform providers that support the App.

  • Vault and app-lock features are intended to add local protection for private files.
  • Biometric and lock features depend on your device capabilities and configuration.
  • No storage, transmission, or security method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Children’s Privacy

The App is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in a way that is intended to target or profile them. If you believe a child has provided personal information in a way that requires deletion or parental action, please contact us.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the App, legal requirements, platform rules, or third-party services. When we make updates, we will revise the effective or updated date on this page and publish the latest version at the active policy URL.
